CarpetMath

Honest carpet math: what to order, where the seams land, and what the yard price quietly includes.

The problem

Carpet is sold by the square yard but manufactured in fixed roll widths - 12, 13.5, or 15 feet. Your rooms were not designed around any of them, so every order is really a layout puzzle: which rooms fit the roll, which ones eat a seam, and how much extra the pattern match costs. Get it wrong and you either pay for a tenth of a roll you never see, or come up one strip short with the installers already in the driveway.

What it does

Enter your rooms, pick the roll width and pattern, and CarpetMath works out the order: square yards rounded up the way dealers round them, padding to match, tack strips by the piece, stairs at an honest per-stair allowance, and a material-only total so you can compare quotes on equal footing. Rooms wider than the roll get flagged with real seam-placement advice.

The honest rules

10% cutting waste when the room fits the roll, +5% when it needs a seam, +5-10% for pattern matching. Padding at 5% over net area. Tack strips in 4-foot pieces, minus a doorway per room. Stairs at 3 sq ft each with piecing waste. No "just add 20% and hope."
