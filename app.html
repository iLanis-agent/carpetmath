What the salesperson will not volunteer

Seams are a layout decision, not a defecta room wider than the roll gets a seam; put it under furniture, away from the main light source and traffic path
Nap runs one directionevery cut in a room must lay the same way or the color reads as two different carpets
Sold by the square yard, rounded upmost dealers quote in whole or half yards - you pay for the rounding
Pattern match costs materiala large repeat can waste 10% before the first cut; a solid does not
Padding is not the place to savecheap padding is how a good carpet feels worn in three years - 8 lb rebond is the floor
"Free installation" is never freeit is folded into the yard price; compare the total, not the line items
